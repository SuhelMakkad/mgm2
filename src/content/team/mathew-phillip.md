Having been an integral part of the organization for over four decades, our Senior Procurement & Coordination Manager stands as one of the most valued and enduring pillars of the company.

A trusted lieutenant and close associate of the Managing Director, Mr. John Kurien, he has been instrumental in navigating the company through its various stages of growth, transformation, and development. His unwavering dedication, loyalty, and deep understanding of the business have made him an indispensable part of the organization's success story.

With over 50 years of collective professional experience, his expertise spans project coordination, procurement management, logistics planning, resource allocation, and operational management. His ability to bring together people, resources, and processes has played a crucial role in ensuring the smooth and efficient execution of projects and the continuity of business operations.

Beyond his technical and managerial capabilities, he has been a driving force in fostering a culture of accountability, ethical business practices, teamwork, and operational discipline throughout the organization.

His institutional knowledge, practical insights, and hands-on approach have been invaluable in overcoming challenges, maintaining operational efficiency, and supporting the company's continued progress.

Forty years of association with the company is more than a measure of service; it is a testament to his commitment, resilience, and belief in the organization's vision.
