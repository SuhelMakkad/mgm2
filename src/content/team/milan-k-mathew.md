With approximately 16 years of professional experience, including 14 years as an integral part of the Al Madar family, Mr. Milan K. Mathew has been a driving force behind the company's business development, commercial growth, and operational progress.

A qualified Mechanical Engineer with a diverse range of expertise, Milan brings together technical knowledge, commercial acumen, and a strong understanding of the construction and specialist contracting industry. His involvement spans business development, marketing, sales, public relations, contract administration, and procurement management, working closely with Mr. John Kurien and Mr. Mathew to strengthen the company's business and operational capabilities.

An important link between management, project teams, and clients, Milan plays an active role in identifying new opportunities, developing business relationships, and translating challenging concepts into viable project opportunities. His ability to recognize potential beyond conventional construction requirements has encouraged the company to explore and undertake unconventional, technically demanding, and specialized projects.

His extensive knowledge of decorative coatings, specialist finishes, artistic applications, and themed environments has been particularly valuable in broadening the company's capabilities and opening doors to distinctive project opportunities. Combining his engineering background with an appreciation for specialized finishes and creative applications, he brings a unique perspective to projects that demand both technical precision and creative execution.

Beyond his professional responsibilities, his proactive approach, adaptability, and close involvement across different levels of the organization have made him an important contributor to the company's continued evolution and growth.

Having dedicated 14 years to the Al Madar journey, Milan continues to play a pivotal role in expanding the company's horizons, strengthening its market presence, and encouraging it to take on new challenges.
