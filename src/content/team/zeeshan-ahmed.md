With approximately seven years of professional experience spanning Pakistan and the UAE, Mr. Zeeshan Ahmed has been instrumental in strengthening Al Madar's Health, Safety and Environment (HSE) framework and fostering a culture where safety, compliance, and accountability remain paramount.

Since joining the Al Madar family three years ago, Zeeshan has played a pivotal role in establishing, implementing, and enforcing HSE policies and practices across the organization. His proactive approach to safety management, coupled with his meticulous attention to regulatory and contractual requirements, has contributed significantly to achieving full compliance with the HSE standards and requirements prescribed by our clients.

A highly qualified and academically accomplished professional, Zeeshan brings a distinctive multidisciplinary perspective to his role. His educational qualifications include a Bachelor of Laws (LLB), a Diploma in Engineering, a Master's degree in Political Science, and a Master's degree in International Relations.

Complementing this diverse academic foundation are his specialized professional credentials in occupational health and safety, including an NVQ Level 7 Diploma in Occupational Health and Safety and ISO 45001 Lead Auditor certification.

This combination of technical knowledge, legal understanding, analytical ability, and specialized HSE expertise enables him to approach workplace safety not merely as a compliance requirement, but as an integral part of responsible business operations.

Highly professional in his approach and respected by colleagues, project teams, and clients alike, Zeeshan has established himself as a dependable and valued member of the management team. His ability to translate HSE policies into practical workplace procedures, promote awareness, and maintain consistent safety standards has contributed meaningfully to the company's operational effectiveness.

At Al Madar, we firmly believe that successful construction is measured not only by the quality of what we build, but also by how responsibly we build it. Zeeshan's contribution reinforces this philosophy, ensuring that the well-being of our workforce and everyone associated with our projects remains a priority.
