A distinguished industry veteran and a titan in his own right, Mr. John Kurien brings with him over five decades of experience and an invaluable wealth of knowledge of the UAE's business and construction landscape.

His extensive experience, business acumen, and visionary leadership have been instrumental in steering the company through its various phases of transformation and growth. From its humble beginnings as a specialist coating contractor to its evolution into a maintenance services provider and subsequently a fully-fledged construction company, John's contribution has been pivotal in taking the organization forward by leaps and bounds.

His leadership is deeply rooted in the values of integrity, commitment, perseverance, and an uncompromising pursuit of excellence. His ability to recognize opportunities, navigate challenges, and inspire those around him has helped shape the company's direction and strengthen its position in an increasingly competitive industry.

With a deep understanding of the region and an unwavering commitment to progress, John continues to lead the company with confidence and purpose, building on its proud 47-year legacy while guiding it towards greater achievements.

His experience has shaped our journey, his leadership has strengthened our foundation, and his vision continues to drive our future.
